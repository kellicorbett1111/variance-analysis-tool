Monthly Variance Analysis Automation

Monthly variance analysis is a recurring close task that explains why accounts moved versus the prior month. Management and auditors expect every material or unusual movement to be explained, which confirms the numbers are reasonable and catches errors or misposted entries before the books are final. This tool flags large or unusual movements, ties each one to the voucher detail, and drafts a short explanation from an evidence pack.
Demo runs on scrubbed data only (one entity, USD, income statement). No real organization data is used.
